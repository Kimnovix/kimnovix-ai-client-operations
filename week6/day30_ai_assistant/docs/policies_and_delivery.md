# Store Hours, Payments, Delivery & Return Policy

## Location & Opening Hours
- Store: Uganda Road, near Sosiani Bridge, Eldoret.
- Hours: Monday to Saturday, 07:30 AM to 06:00 PM EAT. Closed on Sundays and public holidays.
- Support Phone / WhatsApp: 0700 412 890
- Support Email: care@novaagrovet.co.ke

## Payment Methods
- M-Pesa Paybill: 400222, Account: Your Phone Number or Invoice Number.
- M-Pesa Buy Goods Till: 814509 (Nova Agro-Vet Eldoret).
- Cash at counter. Credit terms (Net 14 days) apply only to pre-approved commercial farms and co-ops.

## Delivery Schedule & Charges
- Eldoret Town & Kapseret (within 15 km): Same-day delivery for orders paid before 02:00 PM. Fee: KES 300 for orders under 10 bags; FREE for 10+ bags.
- Turbo, Soy, Moiben & Burnt Forest: Next-day morning dispatch (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday). Flat fee: KES 1,200 (free for 40+ bags).
- Kitale & Nakuru Bulk Orders: Dispatched by arranged transport; minimum 50 bags; quote confirmed by phone.

## Returns & Cold-Chain Policy
- Unopened, undamaged feed bags may be returned within 7 calendar days with the original receipt.
- Cold-chain vaccines and opened veterinary bottles cannot be returned once they leave the store cooler, to protect product potency.
- Always carry a cooler box or ice pack when collecting vaccines; we provide ice packs at KES 100 refundable deposit.
