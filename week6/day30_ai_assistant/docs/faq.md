# Frequently Asked Questions - Nova Agro-Vet & Feeds Ltd

Q: How do I qualify for wholesale bulk feed prices?
A: Ordering 20 or more 50 kg bags on a single invoice qualifies automatically for the bulk rate listed in the catalog. Mixed feed types count toward the 20-bag total.

Q: How should I store livestock feed on the farm?
A: Keep bags on wooden pallets at least 15 cm off the floor and away from damp walls to prevent mould and aflatoxin growth. Use stock on a first-in, first-out basis within 60 days of manufacture.

Q: My animals are sick. Can this assistant prescribe medicine or dosages?
A: No. This assistant only provides product availability, store prices, and policies. For sick animals, dosage calculations, or emergency treatment, call our duty veterinary officer on 0700 412 890 immediately.

Q: Do you supply biosecurity disinfectants for pig and poultry farms?
A: Yes. We stock Virkon S (1 kg tubs at KES 4,200) for wheel dips, footbaths, and housing sanitation. Mix at 1:100 dilution (10 g per litre of water) for general farm biosecurity.

Q: Can I pick up my order after paying via M-Pesa?
A: Yes. Present the M-Pesa confirmation SMS and your name at the dispatch counter. Orders paid via Paybill 400222 or Till 814509 are released immediately once verified.
