# AI Customer Complaint Responder
**Automated customer care reply drafting for Kenyan SMEs**
**Price:** KES 35,000 one-time setup + KES 4,500/month retainer

### What It Does
Turns heated customer complaints from WhatsApp, SMS or email into calm, polite, policy-compliant reply drafts in seconds, in the language the customer wrote in (English, Swahili, or a Sheng-English mix). It runs on the office computer in the web browser, started with a double-click (`run.bat`), and is branded with your company name, support number, follow-up window, brand colour and escalation policy, all stored in one settings file (`config.env`). A private cloud-hosted version for several branches can be quoted separately.

### Who It Is For
Kenyan SMEs handling daily customer complaints about deliveries, orders or service: courier companies, e-commerce shops, Airbnbs, veterinary clinics, restaurants and hardware depots (1 to 20 staff).

### What It Replaces
10 to 15 minutes per difficult complaint spent drafting a careful reply, plus the business lost when stressed frontline staff send defensive messages or promise refunds nobody approved. At 15 difficult complaints a week, that is roughly 12 hours a month of supervisor time. AI usage costs about KES 0.02 to 0.05 per draft on the default model.

### Deliverables
- Branded Complaint Responder application (`app.py` + browser interface), installed and tested on your computer
- Editable `config.env` settings file (change company name, phone, brand colour or refund policy without touching code)
- Double-click Windows launcher (`run.bat`) that installs its own components on first run, plus a plain-English setup and troubleshooting guide
- 1 month of onboarding support

### Monthly Retainer Covers
Model updates when Anthropic releases or retires models, policy and tone tuning, monthly log checks (cost, errors, refusals), and up to 2,500 drafts a month of AI usage.

### Privacy
Complaint text is sent to Anthropic's AI service only to draft the reply and is not stored on the computer. The local log records times, message lengths and cost only.
